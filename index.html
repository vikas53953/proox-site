Proox Private copilot

A second seat at the trading desk, on WhatsApp.

Proox watches the Indian index options market and talks to its owner over WhatsApp. It keeps notes, reminders and a trading journal. It advises one person and it never touches an order.

What Proox does

  • Reads market prices from a read-only data feed.
  • Sends its owner short notes, alerts and reminders on WhatsApp.
  • Keeps a tamper-evident journal of what was said and when.
  • Goes silent the moment its owner sends the word KILL.

What Proox never does

  • It never places, changes or cancels an order.
  • It has no connection to any broker account.
  • It does not message anyone except its owner.
  • It does not offer investment advice to the public.

Privacy

Proox is a single-owner tool. It only answers WhatsApp messages from its owner's own number and ignores every other sender.

Messages are processed to produce a reply and are stored in the owner's private database. They are not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. Messages travel through the WhatsApp Business Platform, which is operated by Meta.
